babocar

A self-driving model car, 2019 – 2021

  • GitHub
  • Monitor app

I designed and built everything on this car: the circuit boards, the 3D-printed parts, the firmware and the debugging tools. It follows lines on the floor, finds its way through a line maze and overtakes on a race track. It holds the all-time lap record at RobonAUT, the autonomous model car race at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics.

How it works

The work is split across several STM32 microcontrollers running FreeRTOS, which talk to each other over a CAN bus:

  • The motor controller ramps the drive motor and the front and rear steering servos to their targets, and reports actual speed and steering.
  • The control panel steers the car along a trajectory: a predefined route, a planner's output, or the lines on the floor. It also runs the maze solver and the race track maneuvers.
  • Front and rear line detectors and distance sensors feed it what the car sees.

Shared code lives in micro-utils. Its hardware abstraction layer has mock implementations, so the firmware logic is unit-tested on a laptop rather than on the car.

Monitoring

A Raspberry Pi on the car runs Node.js services connected through MQTT, with a React UI for watching the car's position, speed and logs and for tuning parameters live. Test drives can be recorded and replayed.

The repository also holds my MSc thesis, Development of Navigation Methods in Dynamic Environment for an Intelligent Model Car.
