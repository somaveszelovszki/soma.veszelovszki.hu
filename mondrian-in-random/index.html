Mondrian in Random

An Android app that generates random compositions in the style of Piet Mondrian and sets them as your home screen or lock screen wallpaper.

What it does

Tap Generate and the app draws a new composition sized to your screen: black grid lines and blocks of primary color, different every time. Pick a light or dark theme, keep generating until you like one, then tap Set as… to apply it to the home screen, the lock screen, or both.

How the pictures are generated

The generator works in two steps. A line generator places horizontal and vertical lines one at a time. Each new line starts and ends on lines that already exist, so the grid always holds together the way it does in a real Mondrian. An image generator then splits the canvas into the rectangles those lines enclose, fills some of them with color, and renders the result to a bitmap at the device's resolution.

The UI is built with Jetpack Compose, and settings such as the theme and the wallpaper targets are saved between launches.
