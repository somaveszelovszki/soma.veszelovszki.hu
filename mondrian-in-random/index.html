Mondrian in Random

An Android app that generates random Mondrian-style compositions and sets them as your wallpaper.

View on GitHub
Platform
Android 8.0+
Language
Kotlin
UI
Jetpack Compose
Year
2023

How it works

Tap Generate for a new composition sized to your screen, in a light or dark theme, then Set as… to apply it to the home screen, lock screen or both.

Lines are placed one at a time, each starting and ending on an existing line, so the grid always holds together. The enclosed rectangles are then filled with color and drawn to a bitmap at the device's resolution.

Fun fact

Mondrian is an anagram of in random. Can you rearrange the letters?

Drag the letters, or tap two to swap them.
