I’ve always been driven by building things — from physical models to production software.

I grew up and currently live in Hungary. Early on, I developed a strong interest in building mechanical systems, starting with complex LEGO models, particularly cars. This interest naturally evolved into engineering projects, including a self-driving model car as the focus of my Master’s thesis.

While studying at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics, I discovered that software engineering offers a similar kind of creative problem-solving — assembling complex systems from well-designed components. That realization led me to pursue software development professionally, a field I’ve been deeply engaged in ever since.

Over the course of my career, I’ve accumulated 10 years of professional engineering experience in startup environments. While I have experience building web and mobile applications, my primary focus has been on delivering end-to-end product features and developing scalable backend and full-stack systems using TypeScript and C++, with strong ownership from design through production.
